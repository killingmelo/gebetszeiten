Prayer Times is a deliberately minimal app for Android and Wear OS, optimised
for three things: times you can rely on, privacy, and battery life.

OFFICIAL TIMES OR NONE AT ALL

The app never shows a prayer time it cannot vouch for. It fetches the official
times published by Turkey's Diyanet and checks them against two independent
sources; if the sources disagree, it says so. When nothing official exists for a
day, you get a clear notice instead of a number — no quietly substituted
estimate.

If you would rather have an estimate than nothing, a setting turns on a
fallback: the app then computes those days itself (Diyanet method, Fajr 18°,
Isha 17°). Official times still always win.

Official tables for Germany are bundled with the app. They work without a
network and cover more than 940 places.

PRIVACY

No account, no tracking, no ads, no analytics, no location permission — you type
your city, the app never asks where you are. The only thing sent is the identifier
of the place whose times are being fetched; as with any request, the server sees
your IP address. The online sync can be switched off, and the bundled tables take
over.

A variant without the INTERNET permission at all can be built from source.

BATTERY

No polling, no background service, no periodic refresh. The app wakes at prayer
transitions and otherwise stays asleep — roughly a dozen times a day in the
default configuration. The widget updates event-driven.

Optionally the app shows the time remaining until the next prayer as an icon in
the status bar and as a persistent line on the lock screen. That costs more
wake-ups, and the setting says exactly how many.

FEATURES

* Live day overview with progress through the current prayer
* Month view and qibla compass
* Hanafi makruh periods and voluntary prayers (Duha, Awwabin, Tahajjud) —
  optional
* Congregational time (cemaat) for the dawn prayer, lead time adjustable
* Favourite places for switching without searching again
* Hijri date, adjustable text size, high contrast, AMOLED black
* Silent per-prayer reminders (sound and vibration are not the default)
* Home screen widget plus a Wear OS tile and complication

WEAR OS

The watch shows the next prayer as a tile and as a complication. It takes the
times from the paired phone and fetches them itself when needed — for which it
carries its own internet permission and has no separate switch. Without a
network it falls back on the same bundled tables.
