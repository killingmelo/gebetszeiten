Prayer Times is a deliberately minimal app for Android and Wear OS, optimised for
three things: battery life, privacy, and clarity.

Privacy by construction: the default build has neither the INTERNET nor any
location permission. The app technically cannot send anything or track you. You
pick your location manually (a city from an offline list, or coordinates). No
tracking, no ads, no analytics.

Battery-friendly: no polling, no background service, no periodic refresh. The
widget updates event-driven at prayer transitions; on the watch the system
renders the display without waking the app.

Features:
* Offline calculation using the Diyanet method (Fajr 18°, Isha 17°), calibrated
  against the official Diyanet yearly table.
* A running day overview that shows the progress of the current prayer.
* Hanafi makruh times and voluntary prayers (Duha, Awwabin, Tahajjud) – optional.
* Hijri date, adjustable font size and high contrast.
* Silent per-prayer reminders (no sound/vibration by default).
* Home-screen widget and Wear OS tile/complication for the next prayer.

An optional build can fetch the exact official Diyanet times online; only that
build carries the INTERNET permission.
